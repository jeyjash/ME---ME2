About ME & ME

More than what you wear.

ME & ME is a contemporary clothing brand built around simplicity, identity, and considered design.

The Brand

Designed with intention.

ME & ME exists at the intersection of contemporary streetwear and understated design.

We focus on clothing that feels deliberate rather than excessive, balancing strong silhouettes, considered details, and everyday wearability.

Every collection is an exploration of identity, movement, and the relationship between the individual and what they choose to wear.
